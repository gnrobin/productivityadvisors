LWC Productivity Components

Most Sales Cloud orgs make reps leave the record to get anything done. These six Lightning Web Components put the answer, and the next action, in place on the page a rep or manager is already looking at. Each one is built on standard objects, drops onto a Lightning page, and takes a week or less to build.

What In-Place Means

A standard record page shows fields and related lists. It rarely tells a rep whether the deal is healthy, what is missing, or what to do next, so that work moves to reports, spreadsheets and side tools. An in-place component answers one question and offers one action right where the question comes up.

Every component below follows the same rules: it reads data the org already has, it writes back to standard fields so reports keep working, and its thresholds live in settings an admin can change without a developer.

Pipeline

See what moved across the pipeline, then judge each deal on its own.

1

My Pipeline Changes

What moved in the pipeline since a start date: deals that advanced or moved back, slipped or pulled in, grew or shrank, and deals that were added, reopened, won or lost. It replaces the Monday ritual of comparing two report exports. A deal can show more than one row, so a slip and a stage drop on the same deal both appear.

Admin and integration edits are left out by default, so a data cleanup or a bulk load doesn't show up as pipeline movement. Columns sort, and each deal opens in a new tab.

Opportunity and the standard Opportunity History. No field history tracking needed.

Deal Health Scorecard

One card that scores the deal and shows why. Five signals are each rated on track, watch or at risk, so a rep sees what to fix and a manager can scan a deal in seconds.

Scoring is simple on purpose: on track is worth 20 points, watch 10 and at risk 0, for a score out of 100. Under 40 is at risk, 40 to 69 is watch, and 70 or more is healthy. Activity is left out because emails and meetings are often logged to the contact or account, not the deal.

Reads
Opportunity, Opportunity History and contact roles. Next Step age needs a "Next Step updated" date, set by a small flow.
Writes
Nothing. The action button opens the standard dialog.
Tuned by
Thresholds per signal and stage in Custom Metadata

Relationships

Know who you are selling to on the deal, and where you stand with the customer overall.

3

Buying Committee Map

Turns the Contact Roles related list into a picture of the deal team: who is engaged, who has gone quiet, and which roles nobody has filled yet. The Add contact role button makes it quick for reps to fill the gaps.

Works best as a Contacts tab on the Opportunity, with the map sitting above the Contact Roles related list. Agree on the role values first: the standard Role picklist is editable, and the map only helps if everyone uses the same roles.

Opportunity contact roles, last activity per contact, account contacts for suggestions

Tuned by

Account 360 Header

The five numbers anyone opening an account wants first, in one strip at the top of the page. Each tile links to the records behind it.

This one is built to fit each org. ARR might come from Contracts, Assets or a renewal opportunity, depending on what the org tracks, so the tiles and their sources are chosen during the build.
